About VeiVakt

VeiVakt AS is a Norwegian technology company developing sensors and artificial intelligence for warning and monitoring along roads, railways and critical infrastructure.

The system is being built for polar night, snow, cold and places where people are few and far between. The company is based in Lillestrøm and is an industry partner in the WILDETECT research project, led by SINTEF and funded by the Research Council of Norway.

THE INVENTION

VeiVakt moves detection from the car out to the road.

Designed to see what the car cannot

The unit stands at a fixed point beside the road, two to five metres above it, and is designed to watch where the car has no line of sight, such as beyond a bend or over a crest.

Designed to decide on site

All AI analysis runs locally on the device. The local warning is designed to go out at once, without relying on a data connection.

Designed to share what it finds

Hazards are to be shared with traffic management and with connected and autonomous vehicles. After an accident, the system will be able to alert the emergency services.
