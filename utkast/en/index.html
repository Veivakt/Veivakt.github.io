Share

You can start small

You do not have to buy anything to get started. Each step is meant to be worth it for you, even if the next one never comes.

  1. 1 Letter of support

    Free. You confirm that the need exists.

  2. 2 Letter of intent

    Non-binding. For public bodies it gives no advantage in any later tender.

  3. 3 Risk assessment

    Paid assignment. We work out where warning would have the greatest effect.

  4. 4 Pilot

    Paid pilot on your site, with measured results.

  5. 5 Purchase or subscription

    Once the pilot has shown results, under the procurement rules that apply to you.

Before a pilot starts, we agree on five things

Take the first step
The use case
One stretch or site, and one concrete problem.
Success criteria
What counts as detected, and how many false alarms are acceptable.
Cost and benefit
What the pilot must show to justify going further.
Legal and safety review
Permits, approval from the signing authority, and privacy.
Winter and upkeep
Operation in cold and snow, and what maintenance it takes.
